How it works
Arctic Shift scans the selected period. Repeated moderator/automation boilerplate and placeholder content are removed before analysis. OpenAI embeddings cluster a representative multilingual sample, then Nano labels each cluster with a concrete subject and summarizes opinions, disagreements, and sentiment. Generic community words are treated as context rather than topics. The app maps those topics back to the wider filtered archive for discussion volume, leading voices, and popular posts.
