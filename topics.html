Analyze subreddit

Archive-first analysis works best over focused date ranges. Each run currently supports up to 31 days.

How it works
Arctic Shift scans posts and comments in the selected period. OpenAI clusters a representative archive sample into major topics and recurring opinions. The app then maps the full retrieved archive back to those topics for counts, sentiment, authors, commenters, and popular posts.

Coverage & caveats

This page is archive-first: OpenAI identifies and summarizes topics from a representative Arctic Shift sample, while full retrieved archive rows are used for topic counts, sentiment, leading voices, and post popularity. Results are broad but not an exhaustive census of Reddit.
