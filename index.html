Distinct Reddit Authors Dashboard

A mobile-friendly dashboard for subreddit activity. It tracks distinct contributors, returning authors, posting and commenting volume, contribution frequency over time, and aggregated period summaries from Arctic Shift's public Reddit archive.

Definitions
Distinct authors: unique usernames who posted or commented in the selected period.
Returning authors: authors with 2 or more total contributions (posts + comments) in the selected period.
Monthly active authors: unique active usernames in each month bucket.
