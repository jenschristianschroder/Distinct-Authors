How retrieval works
OpenAI searches Reddit from multiple lexical and semantic angles. Arctic Shift then scans the selected date range and backfills matching posts plus comments from matched discussion threads. Results are deduplicated before analytics. The displayed post/comment totals are the analyzed sample and may be capped by “Max per type”; the coverage panel reports larger archive counts when available.

Retrieval log & web sources
Retrieval combines OpenAI web search restricted to Reddit, best-effort direct Reddit thread fetching, and Arctic Shift archive backfill. Coverage is intentionally broad but not exhaustive. Sentiment labels are lightweight local heuristics; the opinion summary is generated by OpenAI from the retrieved sample.
