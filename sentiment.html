Subreddit Sentiment & Opinion Analysis

Explore how a subreddit talks about a topic: sentiment mix, major authors and commenters, recurring opinions, changes over time, and how sentiment differs between popular and less-popular posts.

How sentiment is scored
Sentiment is calculated locally in your browser with a lightweight word/phrase lexicon. It is directional, not a substitute for human reading. The LLM summary is optional and uses representative excerpts from the retrieved sample.
LLM summary settings

The key is not written to the repository or localStorage. Your selected excerpts are sent directly from this browser tab to the endpoint you enter.
